Section A · 主要练习

Q1 定义辨析

(a) A person trades in a car when buying another. Is the used car a medium of exchange? Is this a barter transaction? (b) Could you tell by watching someone buying mints (white discs) with coins (bronze discs) which one is money?

参考答案

(a) No. A medium of exchange can be traded INDEFINITELY and will MAINTAIN ITS VALUE. Neither of these is true of the car. Nor is it pure barter unless the new car has the same monetary value as the old.

(b) You would need to watch which was CONSUMED and which used for FURTHER TRADE.

🎯 (b) 是一道很妙的概念题 单看一次交易，白圆片换铜圆片，无法分辨谁是货币。 判据在于 generally accepted means of payment 中的 "generally"：
被吃掉的是商品；被继续用于下一次交易的才是货币。
这正好呼应 Lecture 4 §1 的定义要素：货币必须能反复流通且保值。

Q2 辨析

Why are these statements wrong? (a) Since their liabilities equal their assets, banks cannot create anything. (b) The money supply has risen because of tax evasion — since cash is untraceable, people are putting less in the banks.

参考答案

(a) Banks create credit (bank deposits) from their cash reserves and liquid assets. By simultaneously expanding BOTH SIDES of their balance sheet, banks increase the money supply.

(b) The opposite would be the case. If people put LESS cash in banks, banks are LESS able to multiply reserves into deposits.

(b) 用公式验证 少存现金进银行 ⇒ cp（公众的现金/存款比）上升 货币乘数 = (cp + 1)/(cp + cb) 因为 cb < 1，cp 上升会使分母增加的幅度大于分子 ⇒ 货币乘数下降 ⇒ 给定 H 下 货币供给下降 极端验证：cp → ∞ 时，乘数 → 1（全是现金，银行完全无法创造存款）
💡 (a) 的关键在 "both sides" 银行放贷时，资产端增加一笔贷款，负债端同时增加一笔存款—— 资产负债表两边同时扩张，账仍然平，但货币供给增加了。 "资产 = 负债"并不妨碍创造货币。

Q3 选择

Saying that banks had become "too big to fail" means: (a) large banks are safer. (b) large banks are less safe. (c) managers of large banks realize they can take risks because politicians will have to bail them out if things go wrong.

参考答案

(c) Since politicians will bail out banks that are too big to fail, managers at these banks realize that they do not have to be very cautious with taking risk.

🎯 这就是 moral hazard（道德风险） 与 Lecture 5 §2 的设计对照着看： 政府补偿存款人（防止恐慌）但不补偿股东（保持管理层警觉）。
"Too big to fail" 恰恰破坏了后半条——如果连股东都被救，纪律机制就失灵了。

Q4 重点 计算

Suppose sight deposits = £30, time deposits = £60, banks' cash reserves = £2, currency in circulation = £12, building society deposits = £20. Calculate M0 and M4.

参考答案
M0 is cash in circulation, so M0 = £12 M4 = cash in circulation + sight deposits + time deposits + building society deposits = 12 + 30 + 60 + 20 = £122
⚠️ 陷阱：银行的 £2 现金准备不计入任何货币供给口径 题目给了 banks' cash reserves = £2，它既不进 M0 也不进 M4。
原因（Lecture 4 §6）：
• 货币供给 M = 流通中现金 + 存款 —— 银行金库里的现金不在流通中；
• 货币基础 H = 现金 + 准备金 —— 准备金进的是货币基础，不是货币供给。

顺手算一下：H = 12 + 2 = £14；货币乘数 = M4/H = 122/14 ≈ 8.7。

Q5 选择

After the financial crash, bank lending to the private sector slumped because… (a) new regulations were introduced (b) banks were broken up (c) banks thought prospective borrowers were too risky (d) the value of bank reserves had fallen (e) only a and d (f) only c and d.

参考答案

(a), (c) and (d) are ALL true. New regulations were introduced, banks thought prospective borrowers were risky, and the value of bank reserves had fallen. All this reduced bank lending to the private sector.

💡 三条原因对应三个机制 (a) 监管 ⇒ 法定准备金要求上升 ⇒ cb↑；
(c) 借款人风险高 ⇒ 银行主动持有更多超额准备金 ⇒ cb↑；
(d) 准备金价值下跌 ⇒ H 下降。
三者共同使 M = 乘数 × H 大幅下降——这正是 Lecture 5 §8 QE 案例的背景。

Q6 概念

Initially gold coins were used as money but people could melt them down and use the gold for industrial purposes. (a) What must have been the relative value of gold in these two uses? (b) When could gold become a token money? (c) When could gold disappear from monetary circulation completely?

参考答案

(a) They would have had EQUAL value. If not, then holders would have melted down the one of lower value to make the one of higher value.

(b) It would require an official stamp (e.g. a picture of the head of an emperor or king or queen was — and still is — a common feature on coinage) which would make it MORE valuable as money than for other purposes.

(c) When gold becomes LESS valuable as money — for example, when more efficient TOKEN money is used.

🎯 (a) 的论证是标准的"套利论证" 若两种用途价值不等，就存在套利机会；套利会一直持续到两者相等为止。 这类"无套利均衡"论证在经济学答题中很拿分。

Q7 概念

Suppose banks raise interest rates on time deposits whenever rates on bank loans rise. Does a rise in the general level of interest rates have a big or small effect on the demand for time deposits?

参考答案

A rise in the general level of interest rates is likely to have a SMALL effect on the demand for time deposits. Although in theory a higher rate of interest makes saving in time deposits more attractive, this "substitution effect" is often outweighed by other factors. For example, if the saving is for a specific purchase or purpose, a FALL in interest rates could cause a household to save MORE, in order to reach the desired goal. Households often save more when they are uncertain about the future, regardless of the interest rate.

💡 本题的深层考点：广义货币的机会成本是利差 Lecture 4 §9：持有广义货币的机会成本是 Rb − Rd。
题目说"银行贷款利率上升时定期存款利率也随之上升" ⇒ 利差基本不变 ⇒ 持有定期存款的机会成本几乎没变 ⇒ 需求变化很小。
这是比参考答案更精确的解释，写上去会加分。

Q8 机制

How do commercial banks create money?

参考答案

Commercial banks know that only a small percentage of depositors will demand their cash at any one time. Hence, they can simultaneously create loans and deposits to match, WITHOUT requiring a further cash injection. They can do this only to the "limit of prudence" determined by the ratio of transactions by value carried out in cash in the economy. This is the cash/deposits ratio.

🎯 三个关键词一个都不能少 ① 只有一小部分储户会同时取现（fractional reserve 的前提）；
② 贷款与存款同时被创造（不需要额外注入现金）；
③ 受"审慎限度"约束，即现金/存款比率。

Q9 重点 计算

Banks wish to hold reserves R = 1% of deposits D; the public wish to hold cash C = 2% of deposits. Monetary base H = C + R; broad money = cash with the public plus deposits. What is the value of the money multiplier?

参考答案
cb = 0.01（银行的准备金/存款比） cp = 0.02（公众的现金/存款比） M/H = (cp + 1) / (cp + cb) = (0.02 + 1) / (0.02 + 0.01) = 1.02 / 0.03 = 34
💡 检查你是否真的理解了这个公式 若 cp = 0（公众完全不持现金），乘数 = 1/cb = 100。
实际只有 34——公众区区 2% 的现金偏好，就把乘数从 100 砍到 34。
因为漏到公众手里的现金无法被再次放贷，创造链条被打断了。
这个对比在 essay 里说明"为什么央行控制不了货币供给"非常有力。
推导（不要死记公式，会推更保险） R = cbD，C = cpD H = C + R = (cp + cb)D M = C + D = (cp + 1)D ⇒ 两式相除，D 约掉：M/H = (cp + 1)/(cp + cb)

Q10 · Q11 概念

Q10：信用卡能用来支付，为什么不算货币？

Credit cards allow you TEMPORARILY to POSTPONE a payment, but eventually the money — which is likely to be a BANK DEPOSIT — will be transferred from the purchaser's bank account to that of the credit card company (plus a great deal of interest). But it is the BANK DEPOSITS (or, sometimes, cash) and NOT the credit card, which are money.

🎯 一句话记住 信用卡是"获取信贷的手段"，不是"支付手段本身"。 真正完成支付的是银行存款。
但信用卡确实影响货币供给——通过降低 cp（减少现金使用）⇒ 提高货币乘数 （Lecture 4 §7 明确提到这一点）。
Q11：旅行支票 / 学生铁路卡 / 信用卡该计入货币供给吗？

(a) Travellers' cheques should NOT be included in measures of the money supply as they are not retraded repeatedly in Britain.

(b) No. Rail cards secure a LOWER PRICE for the holder, so they increase the holder's spending power, but they are NOT money.

(c) Like cheques, credit cards give ACCESS to credit (money) but they are not themselves money. It is the bank deposits transferred — by cheque from one person to another, or, in the case of credit cards, from the spender to the credit card company — that are money. Cards REDUCE the demand for cash, so they may reduce the cash/deposits ratio, but they are not themselves part of the money supply. Also, credit card stubs cannot be reused to purchase other goods.

💡 三题共用同一个判据 回到定义：generally accepted means of payment。
• 旅行支票：不能反复流通（not retraded repeatedly）；
• 铁路卡：提高购买力 ≠ 是货币；
• 信用卡：是获取货币的通道，不是货币（凭条不能再拿去买别的东西）。

Q12 Essay

Lots of institutions accept deposits and reissue them on demand — building societies, Christmas savings clubs and theatre cloakrooms. What is the key feature of banks that distinguishes them from other institutions? Why does this matter?

参考答案（参考解答给了极完整的提纲，是本 tutorial 的重头戏）

第一部分：两个类比为什么不成立

类比差异（参考原文要点）
剧院衣帽间 • Not all deposits are the same. 大衣款式尺码各异，而银行里一单位货币与另一单位完全相同 （money is homogeneous）
• 借大衣的人要的是大衣本身，不是寄存票；而借钱的人只是把钱当作交换媒介—— 因此只要交易者对银行有信心，银行的"付款承诺"（支票）就和现金一样好
• 所有寄存票持有者都会在演出结束时来取大衣；而经验表明 任何时点只有一小部分货币持有者要求提取现金 （2007 年困境之前，英国经济中按价值计不到 2%）
圣诞储蓄俱乐部 组织者可能在圣诞前很早的时期发放少量贷款，但必须非常小心地在圣诞临近时收回全部贷款、 备好现金。因此这类组织者愿意冒险的话，确实可能在短期内使货币供给增加极小的量。
相比之下，正常时期公众对现金的需求在全年分布相当均匀。 现金是现代经济的零钱，发行当局会在圣诞与假期等需求高峰增加流通量。 但随着借记卡与信用卡连小额交易也被广泛使用，这一点已无足轻重。

第二部分：银行区别于其他机构的两个关键特征

🎯 这两条就是本题的核心答案 ① 在银行的存款可以被用作 medium of exchange 与 store of value；
② 只需要极小一部分存入的现金（高能货币）来支撑交易。
特征参考解答展开
① 存款本身就是货币 • 在高度发达的经济中，现金主要用于小额交易（公交车费、糖果、香烟—— 而即使这些现在也常用信用卡/借记卡完成，我们可能正走向无现金社会）
• 多数人的工资、养老金、利息、股息、福利与其他收入现在都通过支票或银行转账付入银行账户
• 同样，多数大额支付（按揭、水电费、假期、汽车与白色家电等昂贵物品） 都通过直接借记、长期委托或支票从银行账户直接支付
• 这些转移不涉及任何现金的移动，只是"纸上数字的变化"（如今是磁盘上的位）—— 收款人余额上升、付款人余额下降
• These changes in bank balances, or bank credit, or bank deposits, are TODAY'S MONEY.
② 部分准备金放大 参考解答给的算例（假设按价值计的现金交易比例为 10%）：
• 若 £10m 现金存入银行，正常情况下只有 10% 会被取现（同时又被收款人存回）
• 银行会发现自己有 £9m 闲置
• 作为利润最大化者，银行可以用这些闲置余额向其他客户放贷或自行购买证券
• 银行可以这样做到"审慎水平"，创造出 £90m 的存款
• 因此银行能以 £10m 现金支撑 £100m 的存款
• 只要不超过这一审慎水平、且现金/存款交易比率维持在 10%，银行就是安全的

第三部分：几点现实与近期发展

  • Banks keep other LIQUID ASSETS in addition to cash. The amount or ratio is sometimes laid down by the government.
  • If the bank exceeds the level of prudence, this will result in more customers taking cash out than depositors are putting in.
  • This can lead to panic and a "run on the bank." This was thought to be unlikely in Britain until it actually happened with Northern Rock in 2008.
  • The crash of confidence at that time arose out of the banks investing too much in securities which turned out to be worthless.
  • Many felt this was the result of the cult of "deregulation" practised from the 1980s onwards, particularly in Britain and America.
  • Reforms, including greater regulation by governments, and the splitting of the "retail" functions of banks from their gambling, or merchant, side, have been proposed.

第四部分：Why does this matter?（结论段）

A well-functioning banking system is VITAL for the proper functioning of a modern economy. Businesses produce IN ADVANCE of demand (and therefore income), so they often need to borrow in order to produce the goods they will eventually sell. Some large companies can finance this investment out of profits, but new and many small companies can't.

Similarly households often need to borrow, particularly to buy a house. If people had to save up before they could buy a house, they could be well advanced in years before they could obtain a family home. Some would argue that potential house buyers ought to save a proportion of the purchase price first. It is questionable whether households actually need to borrow for new furniture and gadgetry, but that is today's custom, and some would argue that this "live now pay later" attitude is necessary to keep up demand and full employment.

💡 essay 结构提示 这份参考答案完美示范了 Define → Analyse → Conclude：
用反例（衣帽间、储蓄俱乐部）界定概念 → 提炼两个关键特征 → 用算例量化 → 补充现实与改革 → 最后回答 "why does this matter"（对实体经济的意义）。

Q13：金融危机对广义货币供求的影响（三问）

(a) With a VERTICAL money supply schedule, the intersection of supply and demand determines the equilibrium interest rate at this quantity of money.

(b) The crash had an AMBIGUOUS effect on money DEMAND — people may have switched from risky assets into safer assets（使需求上升）but may also have worried that banks themselves had become risky（使需求下降）— so the LARGER effect was the LEFTWARD SHIFT IN BROAD MONEY SUPPLY as the banks rapidly contracted their willingness to supply bank deposits.

(c) Without substantial creation of narrow money, equilibrium interest rates would have INCREASED precisely at the time of a sharp recession. Monetary policy was used to try to shift the money supply schedule to the RIGHT again.

🎯 (b) 的"ambiguous"是本题最精彩的地方 危机对需求的影响方向不确定（避险 ⇒ 需求↑；怕银行倒 ⇒ 需求↓）， 但对供给的影响方向明确（左移）且幅度更大。
答题时明确区分"方向确定的效应"与"方向不确定的效应"，是很高级的写法。
Q14：如果对金融部门健康的信心大幅上升会怎样？货币政策会如何回应？

Banks would increase preparedness to create bank deposits and lend to customers. BROAD money supply would increase. Monetary policy will wish to WITHDRAW the substantial cash it has previously injected, which results in a dramatic change in the COMPOSITION of broad money between narrow money and bank deposits.

💡 这题就是 QE 的"退出"（quantitative tightening） 信心恢复 ⇒ cb 下降 ⇒ 货币乘数回升。
若央行不回收之前注入的 H，M = 乘数 × H 会暴涨。所以必须缩表。
结果是 广义货币的"构成"改变：窄币（准备金）份额下降，银行存款份额上升—— 回到危机前的正常状态。

Section B · 计算与选择

B4 重点 计算

银行持有存款的 5% 作为现金准备；公众持有的通行货币等于其银行存款的 四分之一； 高能货币存量 = £12 million。（保留两位小数）

参考答案（七小问全解）
(a) 初始货币乘数 cp = 0.25（公众的现金/存款比），cb = 0.05（银行的准备金/存款比） Money multiplier = (cp + 1)/(cp + cb) = (0.25 + 1)/(0.25 + 0.05) = 1.25/0.30 = 4.17
(b) 货币存量 M = 乘数 × H = 4.17 × 12 = 50.04
(c)(d) 银行只需持有 4% 时 乘数 = 1.25/0.29 = 4.31 M = 4.31 × 12 = 51.72 ⇒ cb↓ ⇒ 乘数↑ ⇒ M↑ ✓
(e)(f) 银行回到 5%，但公众现金比升到 30% 乘数 = 1.30/0.35 = 3.71 M = 3.71 × 12 = 44.52 ⇒ cp↑ ⇒ 乘数↓ ⇒ M↓ ✓

(g) It is clear that BOTH cp and cb influence the size of the money stock. The question is WHETHER EITHER OF THESE RATIOS CAN BE INFLUENCED BY POLICY ACTION. The alternative is to operate on the stock of HIGH-POWERED MONEY itself.

🎯 (g) 是全题的落点，也是 Lecture 4 §7 的核心政策含义 cp 与 cb 由私人行为决定，央行很难直接控制。
因此央行的现实选择是 操作 H（通过 OMO）——但这样一来， M 的变化就取决于一个央行无法掌控的乘数。
这就是"为什么现代央行以利率而非货币量为工具"的完整论证（Lecture 5 §6–7）。
💡 三组数字的对比很值得记 cb: 5%→4%（降 1 个百分点）⇒ M 从 50.04 升到 51.72（+3.4%）
cp: 25%→30%（升 5 个百分点）⇒ M 从 50.04 降到 44.52（−11%）
⇒ 公众的现金偏好对货币供给的影响相当大。

B5 重点 机制

The competition amongst banks to offer customers loans intensifies. What are the implications for the money supply?

参考答案

If competition intensifies, then the LOAN rate is likely to FALL and the SAVINGS rate could RISE. More funds will come into the banking sector and more loans will be made, leading to more deposit creation and a FASTER EXPANSION of the money supply.

用 Lecture 4 §7 的框架翻译一遍 竞争加剧 ⇒ 贷款利率↓、存款利率↑ ⇒ 利差（interest rate spread）缩窄 ⚠️ 注意课件 Slide 24 的原话： "The higher the interest rate spread, the more banks wish to lend and the more they risk a low ratio of cash reserves to deposits." ⇒ 利差缩窄本身会使银行放贷意愿下降（cb 上升） 但参考答案强调的是另一条更强的渠道： 存款利率上升 ⇒ 更多资金流入银行体系（cp 下降） 贷款利率下降 ⇒ 贷款需求上升，放贷更多 ⇒ 更多存款创造 ⇒ 货币供给更快扩张
💡 两条渠道方向相反，答题时最好都提 净效应取决于哪条渠道更强。参考答案认为 "资金流入 + 放贷增加"占优 ⇒ 货币供给扩张更快。
但补一句 "however, a narrower spread may reduce banks' willingness to lend at any given reserve ratio" 会显示你完整掌握了课件内容。
另参见 Section C Q5：竞争也可能带来负面效应—— 银行可能过度扩张信贷、持有更低准备金，从而在财务上更脆弱。

B9 重点 九问

下列各项分别如何影响对实际货币余额的需求？

参考答案（九问全解 —— 每一问都要点明"通过哪个动机"）
变化对实际货币需求机制（参考答案）
(a) 实际收入增加↑ 通过 transactions 与 precautionary 两个动机共同使需求上升
(b) 对未来的信心增加↓ 若把它解读为不确定性下降，则通过 precautionary motive 的作用使货币需求下降
(c) 持币机会成本增加↓ 减少实际货币需求，主要通过 asset motive
(d) 名义利率下降↑ 这是 (c) 的反面：名义利率代表持币的机会成本
(e) 价格水平上升不变 这会影响名义货币需求，但对实际货币余额的需求不受影响
(f) 风险资产与定期存款之间的利差增加↓ 若考虑广义货币，这个利差同样代表持币的机会成本 ⇒ 预期实际货币需求下降
(g) 未来交易的不确定性增加↑ 通过 precautionary motive 提高实际货币需求
(h) 收入支付频率下降
（如由周薪改为月薪）		 取决于反应 效果取决于人们如何反应：
• 若不改变支出模式，则可能提高实际货币需求；
• 但他们可能选择在货币与债券之间转换资金， 以便让部分时间闲置的现金赚取回报；
• 或者改变支出模式，例如每月去一次冷冻食品店
(i) 英格兰银行通过公开市场操作增加高能货币存量 不移动需求曲线 这一项影响的是货币的供给：随着利率变化，可能有沿着需求曲线的诱发移动， 但不是需求函数本身的移动。
（参考解答注：这种"沿线移动 vs 曲线移动"的区分最早出现在第 3 章。）
🎯 这道题几乎把 Lecture 4 §8–10 全考了 答题模板：「方向 + 通过哪个动机 + 一句机制」。
特别注意三个"非标准"答案：
(e) 价格上升 ⇒ 实际需求不变（这是 Lecture 4 最重要的一句话）；
(h) 方向不确定，要讨论两种可能的反应；
(i) 影响供给不影响需求，只会引起沿 LL 曲线的移动。

B10 重点 概念

The expected return from the stock market falls. What do you expect to happen to the demand for real money balances?

参考答案

Shares are yet another ALTERNATIVE ASSET. So just like bonds, when the return on shares FALLS, the demand for real money balances will INCREASE. (N.B. flows in and out of mutual funds usually coincide with retail investor confidence.)

💡 一句话原理 持币的机会成本 = 放弃的其他资产的回报。 股票预期回报下降 ⇒ 持币的机会成本下降 ⇒ 货币需求上升。
这与 Lecture 4 §8 的 asset motive（把货币作为低风险组合成分）完全一致。

Section C

Section C · 讨论题（选做）

C1：银行业创新（借记卡、网上银行）如何影响信贷创造？

Banking sector innovations lead to more money being held in the banking system. This enables banks to expand credit creation. But, on the other hand, they may mean that money is being used MORE EFFICIENTLY to make payments, and this may reduce the amount of money in the banking system, reducing the ability of banks to expand credit.

💡 两个方向都要写 渠道①：少用现金 ⇒ cp↓ ⇒ 乘数↑；
渠道②：支付效率提高 ⇒ 所需的货币余额减少 ⇒ 存款总量下降。
净效应不确定——这类"两条相反渠道"的答案在英式评分里比单边论断更拿分。
C2：货币需求的决定因素是什么？

The transactions motive reflects the fact that payments and receipts are not synchronized. In an uncertain world, there is also a precautionary motive: in advance, we decide to hold money to meet contingencies that we cannot yet foresee. This demand for money is a demand for REAL money balances and therefore depends on the price of goods. Finally, the asset motive reflects dislike of risk: people sacrifice a high average rate of return to obtain a portfolio with a lower but SAFER rate of return.

The transactions, precautionary and asset motives suggest that there are BENEFITS to holding money. But there is also a COST — the interest forgone by not holding high interest-earning assets instead. People hold money up to the point at which the MARGINAL BENEFIT of holding another pound just equals its MARGINAL COST.

🎯 这段可以直接当作"货币需求"essay 的模板 三大动机（收益侧）→ 机会成本（成本侧）→ MB = MC 的最优条件—— 正是 Lecture 4 §8–9 的结构。
C3：完善的法律与政治制度如何影响货币需求？

On the one hand, well-functioning legal and political systems should reduce legal, political and economic uncertainty, leading to a LOWER precautionary motive for money. On the other hand, they should also stimulate the development of the financial sector, which may INCREASE the demand for money.

C4：DVD 播放机和/或 DVD 能充当货币吗？

DVDs and players could act as a SHORT-TERM store of value, they would be accepted as a MEDIUM OF EXCHANGE and they might even act as a UNIT OF ACCOUNT. Ask students to think why DVD players are stolen from their rooms.

💡 这题的用意 用 Lecture 4 §1 的四大职能逐条检验。
DVD 播放机能满足前三条（短期价值储藏、交换媒介、甚至记账单位）， 但作为 store of value 会因技术过时与磨损而贬值， 且不满足同质性（homogeneity）——所以不是好的货币。
"为什么小偷偷 DVD 播放机"正说明它被普遍接受、易于转手——具备货币的部分特征。
C5：银行业竞争加剧是好事吗？

Increased competition should lead to a narrower interest rate spread, reducing the interest rate on loans and raising the quantity of lending, and raising the interest rate on deposits and hence the quantity of deposits. It may also increase the efficiency of the banking sector. These effects are analogous to the standard effects that increased competition should have on any industry.

But there may be NEGATIVE effects of increased competition in the banking sector: banks may expand credit EXCESSIVELY; they may hold LOWER RESERVES which may make them more exposed financially, and so forth.

🎯 这题与 A12 的"2007 危机成因"呼应 A12 提到危机源于1980 年代以来"去监管崇拜"下银行过度投资于最终变得一文不值的证券。 竞争本身是好的，但金融业的竞争会内生地降低审慎水平—— 这正是为什么金融业需要审慎监管（Lecture 5 §2 的资本充足率）。
C6：为什么金融危机期间存款乘数与货币乘数都崩塌了？

The MONEY multiplier is M/(R + C) — broad money supply M divided by banks' reserves R and cash held by the public C. The bank DEPOSIT multiplier is M/R. During the financial crisis, banks close to insolvency have had to restore their solvency by rebuilding their assets and reserves. The substantial fall in bank lending significantly REDUCED these multipliers — i.e. the ratio of broad money to the monetary base is lower and banks have fewer deposits for every £1 in reserves.

💡 这正是 Lecture 5 §8 QE 案例的机制 课件数据：2008/5–2009/7，英国银行准备金从 £27bn 涨到 £152bn（+£125bn）， 但广义货币只从 £1737bn 涨到 £2001bn（+£264bn）—— 乘数已经从约 64 跌到约 13。
若无 QE，广义货币会跌到原来的六分之一。
Exam Technique

✍️ 本次 Tutorial 的答题结构点评

题型 1：货币乘数计算（A9, B4）

固定四步

① 先写出 R = cbD 与 C = cpD 两个定义；
② 推出 H = (cp+cb)D 与 M = (cp+1)D；
③ 相除得 M/H = (cp+1)/(cp+cb)，代入数字；
④ 再乘 H 得到货币存量，并说一句方向（cb↓ 或 cp↓ ⇒ 乘数↑）。

不要只背公式——B4(g) 明确问的是"政策含义"， 必须能说出 cp 与 cb 由私人行为决定、央行只能操作 H。

题型 2：「这算不算货币？」（A1, A10, A11, C4）

🎯 永远回到定义，用三条判据 Money is any GENERALLY ACCEPTED means of payment for delivery of goods or the settlement of debt.
① 是否被普遍接受（旧车、DVD ✗）；
② 是否能反复流通（旅行支票 ✗、信用卡凭条 ✗）；
③ 是否是"支付手段本身"还是"获取支付手段的通道"（信用卡是通道 ✗）。

并且——即使不是货币，也要说明它如何影响货币供给 （信用卡降低 cp ⇒ 提高乘数）。这一句往往是拉开差距的地方。

题型 3：货币需求的比较静态（B9, B10）

三段式模板

「方向 + 动机 + 机制」，例如：
"An increase in uncertainty about future transactions raises the demand for real money balances through the precautionary motive, because people hold money to meet contingencies they cannot yet foresee."

三个必须记住的特例：
• 价格水平上升 ⇒ 实际需求不变（名义需求按比例上升）；
• 供给侧变化（OMO）⇒ 不移动需求曲线，只引起沿 LL 的移动；
• 方向不确定时（如支付频率变化），要把两种可能的反应都写出来。

题型 4：Essay（A12）

A12 的参考答案示范了一个很好的策略：用"反例"来界定概念。
题目问"银行与其他机构的区别"，答案先把两个类比（衣帽间、储蓄俱乐部）逐条拆解， 在拆解过程中自然浮现出两个关键特征，然后用£10m → £100m 的算例量化， 最后回答 "why does this matter"（企业先生产后销售需要借贷、家庭买房需要按揭）。

💡 可直接套用的结论句 "A well-functioning banking system is vital for the proper functioning of a modern economy, because businesses produce in advance of demand and therefore need to borrow in order to produce the goods they will eventually sell."
