Q1 重点 计算

Car firms buy raw materials (steel), intermediate goods (windscreens, tyres) and labour to make cars. Windscreen and tyre companies hire workers and also buy raw materials from other industries. What is the value added of the car industry (the three firms shown below)?

Producer ofOutputIntermediate goods usedRaw materials usedLabour input
Cars1000250100100
Windscreens1501050
Tyres1001030
参考答案

The value added is the value of the final product less the value of the raw material used. Assuming that "the car industry" includes all three firms, then: 1000 − 120 = 880.

为什么是减 120，不是减 250 + 100 + 10 + 10 把三家企业当作一个"产业"看待： • 挡风玻璃（150）与轮胎（100）是产业内部的交易 ⇒ 不能重复计算 • 只有从产业外部购入的原材料才要扣除：100 + 10 + 10 = 120 • 产业的最终产出 = 汽车 1000 ⇒ Value added = 1000 − 120 = 880 ⚠️ 劳动投入（100 + 50 + 30）不扣除——工资是要素收入，本身就是增加值的组成部分。
⚠️ 最常见的两个错误 ① 把中间品 250 也减掉 ⇒ 那是把产业内部交易重复扣除了；
② 把劳动成本减掉 ⇒ 增加值 = 工资 + 利润 + 租金 + 利息，工资在增加值之内。

Q2 计算

GNP at market prices is £300 billion. Depreciation is £30 billion, indirect taxes £20 billion. (a) What is the national income? (b) Why does depreciation cause a discrepancy between GNP and national income? (c) Why do indirect taxes enter the calculation?

参考答案
(a) Both depreciation and indirect taxes need to be subtracted: 300 − 30 − 20 = £250 billion

(b) National income is net national product at basic prices. Depreciation is part of the cost of producing output（折旧是生产产出的成本的一部分，所以要从 gross 里扣掉才得到 net）。

(c) Indirect taxes do not add to the value of the national income. If they did, nations would be able to become richer and richer simply by taxing more.

💡 (c) 这句反证法很漂亮，值得直接背下来用 "如果间接税算作国民收入，那国家只要不停加税就能越来越富"——用归谬法说明为什么要用基本价格。

Q3

Which of the following statements is correct? (a) Increasing the staff of the police force in response to higher crime raises national income because government spending is higher. (b) Increasing the staff of the police force reduces national income because society wastes resources tackling crime. (c) There is no effect on national income.

参考答案

(a) is correct, because government expenditure in wages (e.g. wages of teachers, soldiers, cleaners, and, as in this question, the police) adds to the national income. The arguments put forward in (b) and (c) are really couched in terms of national well-being or welfare (which may be more important than national income).

🎯 这题考的是"国民收入 ≠ 福利" 警察工资是要素收入，计入 GDP。但更多警察是 Lecture 1 §10 说的 "the production of 'regrettables'"——计入 GDP 却不提升福利。 题目问的是 national income，不是 welfare，所以选 (a)。

Given the data below: (a) What is 2010 GDP in 2009 prices? (b) What is the growth rate of real GDP from 2009 to 2010? (c) What is the inflation rate?

(a) 用 deflator 把名义 GDP 折成 2009 年价格 Real GDP2010 = Nominal GDP2010 × (100 / 110) = 2400 × (100/110) = £2182
(b) 实际 GDP 增长率 2009 年实际 GDP = 2000（因为 deflator = 100，基期） 增长 = 2182 − 2000 = 182 增长率 = 182 / 2000 × 100 = 9.1%

Q5 辨析

Common fallacies. Why are these statements wrong? (a) Unemployment benefit props up national income in years when employment is low. (b) A high per capita real GDP is always a good thing. (c) In 2010, Crummy Movie earned £1 billion more at the box office than Gone With the Wind earned 50 years ago. Crummy Movie is definitely a bigger box office success.

参考答案

(a) Unemployment benefit itself is a transfer payment, since it does not add to real output. However, its expenditure adds to demand and may create the employment of others, so the statement is not completely wrong.

(b) It depends on how the income is distributed. It might conceivably be undesirable if achieved by very unequal income distribution.

(c) The £1 billion has to be adjusted for inflation. In real terms, Gone With The Wind wins by a mile!

🎯 三个错误各对应一个核心概念 (a) 转移支付不计入 GDP；(b) 总量掩盖分配；(c) 名义 vs 实际。
注意 (a) 的答法很讲究：先指出错在哪，再承认它"不完全错"—— 这种"两面兼顾"的写法正是英式评分喜欢的。

Q6 恒等式

Suppose a country is unable to borrow from abroad and must always equate the value of its exports and imports. If the private sector is saving a lot more than it is investing, is the government in surplus or deficit? Why?

参考答案

Since total leakages equal total injections, and since exports equal imports, this means that, if the economy is in equilibrium, the remaining injections (investment + government spending) must be equal to the remaining leakages (saving + taxes). Hence, if saving exceeds investment, government spending must exceed taxes. The private sector surplus is offset by the government deficit.

用 Lecture 1 的恒等式一步到位 (S − I) + (NT − G) ≡ (X − Z) 题目给定 X = Z ⇒ 右边 = 0 ⇒ (S − I) = −(NT − G) = G − NT S ≫ I ⇒ 左边 > 0 ⇒ G > NT ⇒ 政府赤字（deficit）

Q8 重点 计算

Suppose the injections (I, G, X) do not depend on Y, while leakages increase as output increases. (a) Suppose saving S = 0.1Y. If total injections equal 10, what is the equilibrium level of output? (Assume NT = 0 and Z = 0). (b) Now suppose imports Z = 0.4Y, and taxes T = 0.5Y. If G = £40, is the government budget (G − T) in surplus or deficit?

参考答案
(a) 漏出 = 注入 Leakages are always equal to injections: 0.1Y = 10 Y = 100
(b) T = 0.5Y = 0.5 × 100 = 50 G = 40 ⇒ G − T = 40 − 50 = −10 ⇒ 政府盈余（surplus）为 10
⚠️ 注意题目的措辞 题目问的是 "(G − T) in surplus or deficit"。G − T < 0 意味着盈余，不是赤字。 参考答案原文："the government surplus is therefore 10 if G = 40"。
另注意 (b) 沿用 (a) 的 Y = 100（题目未要求重算均衡）。

Q9 计算

GNP = £2000, C = £1700, G = £50 and NX = £40. (a) What is investment I? (b) If exports are 350, what are imports? (c) If depreciation = £130, what is national income? (d) Could net exports be negative? (Assume GNP at market prices; NYA = 0; indirect taxes = 0.)

参考答案
(a) GNP = C + I + G + NX C + G + NX = 1700 + 50 + 40 = 1790 I = 2000 − 1790 = £210 (b) NX = X − Z = 40，X = 350 ⇒ Z = 350 − 40 = £310 (c) 因为 NYA = 0 且间接税 = 0：National income = GNP − depreciation = 2000 − 130 = £1870 (d) Yes, if the value of imports exceeds the value of exports. The economy would have a balance of payments deficit.

Q10 概念

Should these be in a comprehensive measure of GNP: (a) time spent by students in lectures; (b) the income of muggers; (c) the wage paid to traffic wardens; (d) dropping litter?

参考答案

This is a difficult question because "should" implies what ought to happen, a concept economists normally avoid, rather than what actually does.

  • (a) Not included unless the students are in paid employment (e.g. apprentices on day release). The grants of full-time students are transfer payments, not earned income. However, a case could be made for including the time even of full-time grant-less students, since investment in human capital occurs.
  • (b) No — this is simply a "transfer" payment, though if the mugged person claimed on the insurance that would be counted.
  • (c) Yes, they are earnings.
  • (d) Environmentalists argue that pollution should ideally be subtracted from GNP. Paradoxically, however, the wages paid to those who pick it up are counted, so in this way pollution actually ADDS to GNP (as, for example, does the cost of preventing crime).
💡 (d) 是全 tutorial 最值得记的一句 污染本身不减 GDP，清理污染的工资反而加 GDP——这是 Lecture 1 §10 "GDP 不是福利指标"最有力的一个例子。

Q11 · Q13 · Q14 Essay

Q11：电视机价格 30 年不变说明什么？

(i) The nominal price has been constant therefore the REAL price has fallen a lot. (ii) Quality has improved hugely over the period. To explain both of these simultaneously, there must have been a lot of technical progress. Deciding how to revalue goods because of their superior quality is just one of the problems facing a statistician working on the national accounts.

🎯 这题实际考的是"价格指数的质量偏误" CPI 与 deflator 都难以充分调整质量改善 ⇒ 通胀被高估、实际增长被低估。
Q13：如何把闲暇的价值纳入 GDP？

The place to start is that by taking leisure, people reveal that this is at least as valuable to them as working more, provided that they have that choice. For all such people, you would have to decide how many hours a day are the fixed costs of being alive — sleeping, eating, washing — and hence how many hours could potentially be allocated between work and leisure. Knowing something about national wage rates and hours of work, you could have a stab at valuing total leisure hours.

In theory, decisions between work and leisure are made at the margin. Thus in many industries "overtime" is paid at a higher rate in order to make work more attractive than leisure. A person working a 35-hour week would probably value an extra hour's leisure less than someone working a 45-hour week.

Complications: (a) people who don't have the choice — the young, the sick, those caught in a poverty trap; (b) the distinction between those working but choosing some leisure and those on benefit schemes who simultaneously get money and leisure; (c) how to treat parents who stay at home to look after children.

Q14（Essay）：GDP 有多大用处？我们能轻易有更好的指标吗？

The statement in inverted commas is wrong — it is far from pointless to try to measure GDP, provided the result is used with awareness of its limitations. There are many suggestions for constructing a better measure, but none are easy.

参考解答给的三段式提纲（essay 直接照这个骨架写）：

段落要点
① Acknowledge the limitations of GDP • GDP should measure income per head, not just total income
• GDP gives no information about income distribution/equality
• GDP ignores untraded goods and services（housework、voluntary work、own produce、 developing countries 的 subsistence farming、服务互换）
• GDP does not report the informal / "back pocket" economy
• 国际比较应使用 purchasing power parity rate 而非 market rate
② Suggestions for improving GDP • Subtracting for resource use（扣除资源消耗）
• Subtracting for "bads" such as pollution and congestion
③ Attempts at constructing a better measure • UN 的 Human Development Index (HDI)：包含 life expectancy at birth、 mean years of schooling、expected years of schooling、GNI at PPP per capita； 对比较发展中国家特别有用
• 其他可纳入的福祉指标：leisure and working hours、Gini coefficient、 stability of marriages/partnerships、age of retirement、 incidence of mental illness and suicide、% of young people in higher education、 access to theatres/museums/libraries
• "Happiness" itself is difficult to measure, but some of the above are easily quantifiable.
🎯 这道题的完整范例 参见 Lecture 1 页面的答题范例， 以及 Lecture 1 §10 的两大类局限（A 遗漏项 + B 概念不对口）。

B3 计算 （预习 Lecture 2）

In a hypothetical closed economy with no government, planned consumption is 150, planned investment is 50 and total production is 210. (a) Total planned expenditure? (b) Unplanned stock changes? (c) Savings? (d) Actual investment? (e) How would producers react next period?

参考答案
(a) Planned consumption + planned investment = 150 + 50 = 200 (b) Production less expenditure = 210 − 200 = 10 This quantity represents an unplanned addition to inventories. (c) Income less consumption = 210 − 150 = 60 (d) Planned investment plus stock changes = 50 + 10 = 60 Thus actual investment = actual savings ✓

(e) Producers have not sold as much output as they expected and witness an increase in stock levels. Two responses are possible: to reduce output or reduce price. We will initially assume that prices are fixed — so the response to an unplanned increase in stocks will be to reduce output.

🎯 这题是 Lecture 2 的完美预演 它把 actual S ≡ actual I（60 = 60） 与 planned S ≠ planned I（60 ≠ 50） 同时展示出来， 差额恰好是 非计划存货 10。价格固定 ⇒ 产出调整——这就是 45° 线模型的全部机制。

Section C

Section C · 讨论题（选做，但答案很适合当 essay 素材）

C1：更高的 GDP 增长与更低的通胀，哪个宏观目标更重要？

It is misleading to think that one of these objectives is more important than the other. Both are important. It might be argued that there is an optimal rate of economic growth, and an optimal rate of inflation, and the extent to which the government should focus on one rather than the other depends on the extent to which the objectives are being missed — so if inflation is approximately at its optimal rate but growth is severely suboptimal, the government should focus more on growth. A further point is that the objectives may be interrelated — for example, low inflation may be a prerequisite for economic growth.

C2：收入法、支出法、增加值法哪个更可取？

These are three equivalent ways of measuring the same thing, so it is difficult to see how one way can be preferable. It may be easier to measure it in one particular way, possibly because of data availability.

C3：循环流量图与生产可能性边界如何关联？

Both are linked by the scarcity of factor resources. The size of the circular flow of income is determined by the amount and the productivity of factor resources, as is the production possibility frontier. The circular flow moves the analysis on by highlighting the flows of factor resources and incomes, indicating through leakages and injections how the economy might be managed.

C4：为什么人均实际 GDP 比名义 GDP 更准确？

Since it is physical quantities of output that yield people utility, it can be misleading to judge performance by nominal GDP. Real GDP adjusts for inflation by measuring GDP at base-year prices. Per capita GDP measures the standard of living on average. However, it does not capture the degree of equality: the more the income distribution changes over time, the less reliable is the change in per capita real GDP as an indicator of what is happening to any particular person.

C5：应纳入 GNP 的重要因素有哪些？为何 GNP 仍最广泛使用？

GNP ignores non-market activities, "bads" such as pollution, valuable activities such as work in the home, and production unreported by tax evaders. Nor does it measure the value of leisure. However, it is expensive, and sometimes impossible, to make regular and accurate measurements of all these activities. Therefore in practice GNP is the most widely used measure of national performance.

🎯 这个"承认缺陷 → 但解释为何仍在用"的结构，是 essay 结论段的标准写法
C6：瑞士、摩纳哥、百慕大人均 GDP 最高，说明它们增加值更高吗？

All of these countries are tax havens. Therefore the higher GDP per capita probably reflects the level of WEALTH within these economies, rather than any value added.

💡 这题实际考的是 GDP vs GNP 避税天堂里登记的收入并非在当地生产。这类"人在哪 vs 钱记在哪"的问题， 正是 GDP（地域）与 GNP（国籍）区分的现实版本。
Exam Technique

✍️ 本次 Tutorial 的答题结构点评

题型 1：计算题（A1, A4, A8, A9, B1, B2, B7）

写法

① 先写出符号形式的公式（如 GDPmp = C + I + G + X − Z）；
② 再代入数字，中间步骤不能省；
③ 给出带单位的答案（£ million、%）；
④ 一句话解释含义或做一次交叉验证。

B2 就是典型：参考答案第一步是"把项目翻译成课本记号"，这一步本身就是得分点。

题型 2：Why are these statements wrong?（A5, A3, A10）

写法

① 先说清这句话在哪个概念上出错（转移支付 / 名义 vs 实际 / 总量 vs 分配）；
② 给出正确表述；
③ 如果它"部分正确"，一定要说出来——例如 A5(a) 参考答案就明确写 "so the statement is not completely wrong"。
只写"错"而不给出正确表述，拿不到解释分。

题型 3：数据不足型（B1(c)(d)）

🎯 这类题的满分答案有固定套路 "We cannot tell, because we do not know about X."
B1(c)：不知道收入分配；B1(d)：不知道年龄结构与劳动参与率。
明确指出缺少哪一项信息，比含糊地说"不一定"要高一个档次。

题型 4：Essay（A14）

参考答案已经给了三段式提纲——Limitations → Suggestions for improving → Attempts at a better measure。 按 Define → Diagram → Analyse → Conclude 的四段结构套用即可： 定义 GDP 与 welfare 的区别 → （本题可用循环流量图或六栏图）→ 分点论证局限与改进 → 结论："far from pointless, provided the result is used with awareness of its limitations"。
