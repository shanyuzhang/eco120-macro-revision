Section A · 主要练习

Q1 重点 计算

(a) Find equilibrium income when investment demand is 400 and C = 0.8Y. (b) Would output be higher or lower if the consumption function were C = 100 + 0.7Y?

参考答案
(a) Y = C + I Y = 400 + 0.8Y 0.2Y = 400 Y = £2000 (b) Y = C + I Y = 100 + 0.7Y + 400 = 500 + 0.7Y 0.3Y = 500 Y = £1,667 ⇒ Therefore, the output is LOWER.
💡 为什么产出反而更低（值得多写一句） (b) 的自主支出更高（500 > 400），但 MPC 更低（0.7 < 0.8）， 乘数从 5 降到 3.33。乘数下降的效果压过了自主支出的上升。
验算：(a) 400 × 5 = 2000；(b) 500 × 3.33 = 1667 ✓

Q2 选择

Which statement is correct? (a) Any tax is a tax on jobs because it reduces AD. (b) Provided the government spends the tax revenue, the impact of higher spending outweighs the adverse demand effect of higher taxes. (c) Autonomous consumption demand is directly related to consumer confidence. (d) All the above could be true.

参考答案

(d) All the above statements could be true, depending on the other things assumed equal.

💡 为什么 (b) 也可能成立 这就是 平衡预算乘数 = 1（Lecture 3 §7）：ΔG 使 AD 上移全额， 而 ΔT 只使 AD 下移 cΔT ⇒ 净效应仍是扩张。

Q3 机制

Suppose firms are initially surprised by changes in demand. (a) When demand falls, what is the initial effect on stocks? (b) What do firms plan to do to stocks once they can adjust production? Does this reduce or increase the initial fall in demand? (c) Once stocks have been adjusted, what happens to output?

参考答案

(a) There is an unplanned rise in stocks.

(b) Firms plan negative inventory investment to reduce stocks — this provides a second-round reduction in aggregate demand（即：为了消化存货，企业进一步压缩生产， 使 AD 二次下降 —— 加剧了最初的需求下降）。

(c) Once stocks have been adjusted, there is no need to keep planning to reduce them, so planned inventory investment increases (from a negative number to zero). This effect is one of the causes of a subsequent RISE in aggregate demand and hence a CYCLICAL response to the initial shock.

🎯 这题是 Lecture 11 的伏笔 "存货调整完毕后，计划存货投资由负回到零，本身就构成一次需求上升"—— 这正是 Lecture 11 §5 存货波动引起商业周期的机制。

Q4 概念

Could the multiplier ever be less than 1?

参考答案

In the simple model used in this chapter, the multiplier 1/s is always greater than +1, since 0 < s < 1. However, the combined leakages other than savings may be sufficient to reduce the multiplier below unity.

数值验证（用 Lecture 3 的完整乘数） k = 1/[s + ct + z] 若 s = 0.2、c = 0.8、t = 0.5、z = 0.5： 分母 = 0.2 + 0.4 + 0.5 = 1.1 ⇒ k = 0.91 < 1 ✓

Q5 辨析

Why are these statements wrong? (a) If people were prepared to save more, investment would increase and we could get the economy moving again. (b) Lower output leads to lower spending and yet lower output. The economy could spiral downwards forever.

参考答案

(a) This is the essence of the Keynesian revolution. Ultimately, in a closed economy I and S are equal but it is the level of I which determines the level of saving, and NOT vice versa. Higher saving reduces aggregate demand and so the level of economic activity would be further reduced rather than "get moving again".

(b) Because MPC < 1, each fall in output causes a SMALLER fall in demand, so the process eventually comes to a halt.

💡 (b) 的数学本质 每一轮的收缩是上一轮的 c 倍：ΔY = ΔI(1 + c + c² + …)。 因为 0 < c < 1，这个等比级数收敛到 1/(1−c)，不会无限下滑。 这也正是乘数是有限数的原因。

Q6 重点 计算

Economy in equilibrium. Planned investment £100, MPC = 0.6. (a) Investment rises by £30 — what happens to equilibrium output? Now the consumption function changes from C = 0.8Y to C = 0.5Y. (b) What happens to equilibrium income (planned investment being £100)? (c) What happens to the equilibrium proportion of income saved? Explain.

参考答案
(a) The multiplier is 2.5 (= 1/(1 − 0.6)) ⇒ output increases by £30 × 2.5 = £75 (b) When C = 0.8Y：Y = 0.8Y + 100 ⇒ 0.2Y = 100 ⇒ Y = £500 If C falls to 0.5Y：Y = 0.5Y + 100 ⇒ 0.5Y = 100 ⇒ Y = £200 (c) In the absence of government and export/import sectors, S = I. Savings therefore remains unchanged at £100, but which is now equal to 0.5Y.
🎯 (c) 就是节俭悖论的数值版 储蓄倾向从 0.2 升到 0.5（MPS 翻了 1.5 倍）， 但均衡储蓄仍然是 £100——因为 I 外生不变，均衡要求 S = I。
变的是收入：500 → 200。
验算：0.2 × 500 = 100 ✓；0.5 × 200 = 100 ✓ 两者完全相同。

Q7 重点 计算+图

MPC = 0.6, investment demand rises by £30. (a) By how much does equilibrium output increase? (b) How much of that increase is extra consumption demand? Draw the corresponding diagram using planned investment and planned saving, assuming initial output is 100.

参考答案
(a) 乘数 = 1/(1 − 0.6) = 2.5 ⇒ ΔY = 30 × 2.5 = 75 (b) ΔY = ΔI + ΔC ⇒ ΔC = 75 − 30 = 45 （或直接：ΔC = c × ΔY = 0.6 × 75 = 45 ✓） 新均衡产出 = 100 + 75 = 175

参考解答对两张图的文字描述（画图时照这个写标注）：

图参考描述
(a) AD 法 Aggregate demand is an upward sloping line, with positive height when output is zero, and a slope of 0.6. The increase in investment leads to a parallel upward shift in the AD curve by a vertical distance of 30. It now intersects the 45-degree line at an output level that is 75 units higher. Investment increases by 30 and planned consumption by 45.
图上标：截距由 40 升到 70，交点由 100 移到 175。
(b) S = I 法 Investment is a horizontal line and the saving function slopes upward as output increases. A vertical upward shift of 30 in investment now intersects the saving function at an output that is 75 units higher. Again, if investment accounts for 30 of the total increase, planned consumption must account for 45.

Q8 计算

C = 0.75Y and planned investment is 40. (a) Draw the AD schedule. (b) If actual output is 100, what unplanned actions will occur? (c) Equilibrium output? (d) Same answer using planned S = planned I?

参考答案
(a) Intercept = 40, slope = 0.75（AD = 40 + 0.75Y） (b) 产出 100 时：AD = 40 + 75 = 115 > 100 ⇒ 需求超过产出 ⇒ Destocking（非计划存货减少） (c) Y = 40 + 0.75Y ⇒ 0.25Y = 40 ⇒ Y = 160 (d) Yes：S = −0 + 0.25Y，令 S = I： 40 = 0.25Y ⇒ Y = 40/0.25 = 160 ✓ 两法一致
⚠️ 注意"destocking"的方向 AD > Y ⇒ 卖得比产得多 ⇒ 存货被动减少（destocking） ⇒ 企业下期增产。
很多人会把方向记反。口诀：需求旺 → 货被抢光 → 加班生产。

Q9 概念

The diagram shows AD and output. Is there an inflationary gap or a deflationary gap in the economy if the answer is 250?

参考答案

There is a DEFLATIONARY GAP in the economy of £100 billion. The aggregate demand is insufficient to allow an equilibrium output of £250 billion.

💡 两个 gap 的定义（考试要能区分） Deflationary gap（紧缩缺口）：AD 不足以支撑充分就业产出 ⇒ 均衡产出 < Y* ⇒ 失业。
Inflationary gap（通胀缺口）：AD 超过充分就业产出所能提供的 ⇒ 只能推高价格（Lecture 7）。
缺口的大小是垂直距离（AD 的缺口），不是水平距离（产出的缺口）—— 产出缺口 = 缺口 × 乘数。

Q10 进阶

Suppose confidence depends a little on current output: I = aY + I* and C = [A* + bY] + cY. (a) New multiplier? (b) Higher or lower? (c) Is equilibrium output higher?

参考答案
(a) 推导 Y = C + I = A* + bY + cY + aY + I* Y(1 − c − a − b) = I* + A* Y = [I* + A*] / [1 − c − a − b] Multiplier = 1 / [1 − c − a − b] ⚠️ 乘数为正的条件：(a + b + c) < 1

If confidence depends 'too much' on current output, the economy is EXPLOSIVE — a gain in output boosts confidence and demand, leading to further output and confidence gains that keep going indefinitely.

(b) The new multiplier is HIGHER than before（分母从 1−c 变小到 1−c−a−b）。

(c) Whether equilibrium output is higher depends on how I* and A* compare with the previous autonomous values of I and A. If they are the same, the additional confidence effect implies that equilibrium output is HIGHER because of the larger multiplier.

🎯 这题是 Lecture 11 "爆炸性周期"的先声 "信心过度依赖当前产出 ⇒ 经济是爆炸性的"，与 Lecture 11 §4 "explosive cycles that get larger and larger" 是同一个数学结构。

Q11 · Q12 · Q14 Essay

Q11：节俭悖论在什么情况下不成立？

The paradox of thrift relies on output and income adjusting to restore desired injections and desired leakages. In the model explained in this chapter that can always happen. Once we reintroduce aggregate supply, and in particular, a MAXIMUM CAPACITY for the entire economy, it is possible that there is an OUTPUT CEILING above which output and income cannot go. The paradox of thrift might then be frustrated.

💡 完整的三条失效条件 本答案给了供给约束（产出上限）这一条。Lecture 2 §10 还给了另外两条：
② 投资不再外生（储蓄上升压低利率 → 投资上升，IS-MP 框架）；
③ 开放经济（内需下降减少进口，改善贸易余额，部分抵消收缩）。
考试答"何时失效"时，三条一起写。
Q12（Essay）：消费与收入的强相关是否说明"我们本质上都是物质主义者"？

This certainly seems to be the trend in most economies. Recent events in Britain have highlighted the fall in the savings ratio and the accumulation of household debt arising mainly from mortgages, unsecured loans and credit card liabilities. The "boom years" 1997–2007 allowed households benefiting from increased incomes to indulge in instant gratification.

但经济学家提出了其他理论来解释消费模式（这才是本题的得分点）：

  • Wealth effects [when spending exceeds disposable income, net wealth falls] and changes in consumer credit are responsible for most of the shifts in the consumption function. Financing dissaving involves selling off assets and/or borrowing money.
  • Money and interest rates alter consumer spending by affecting the quantity of consumer credit and the interest rates charged on it.
  • The permanent income hypothesis (Milton Friedman) argues that consumption depends on permanent income, not on current or disposable income.
  • The life cycle hypothesis (Franco Modigliani) assumes people make a lifetime consumption plan that is affordable out of lifetime income.
🎯 这题的核心答法 相关不等于因果、也不等于"人性物质主义"。 永久收入假说与生命周期假说说明：消费与当期收入的强相关， 可能只是因为当期收入是永久收入的良好代理，而不是因为人们急于花钱。
这两个假说的详细内容见 Lecture 5 §10。
Q14：衰退中人们想多储蓄，节俭悖论如何解释后果？

The paradox of thrift suggests that a change in the amount households wish to save at each income leads to a change in equilibrium income, but NO change in equilibrium saving, which must still equal planned investment.

If individuals try to save more during recession, consumption decreases, which reduces aggregate demand and thus reduces the total savings in the economy. In equilibrium, planned saving always equals planned investment, and the latter is unaltered. Hence planned saving cannot change. If savings increase, the income falls just enough to equate planned saving and investment at the equilibrium.

In the LONG RUN, once the economy has time to return to potential output, an increase in the desire to save will increase saving and reduce consumption at potential output. Other adjustments may then induce investment demand to rise to restore aggregate demand to its full-employment level. Hence, in the long run, society may BENEFIT from an increase in the desire to save. Investment will rise and the economy's capital stock and potential output may grow more quickly.

🎯 最后这一段是满分与及格的分水岭 短期：储蓄有害（节俭悖论）；长期：储蓄有益（增长）。 这正是 Lecture 10 §7 Solow 模型的结论。 任何"节俭悖论"的 essay 都应该以这一段收尾。

B6 计算

C = A + cY. (a) MPC? (b) How is saving related to income? Given A = 400, c = 0.75, I = 500: (c) At what income is saving zero? (d) Equilibrium income?

参考答案
(a) MPC = c (b) S = Y − C = Y − A − cY ⇒ S = −A + (1 − c)Y (c) 代入：S = −400 + 0.25Y，令 S = 0 ⇒ Y = 1600 (d) C = 400 + 0.75Y，均衡 Y = C + I： Y = 400 + 0.75Y + 500 0.25Y = 900 Y = 3600
⚠️ 参考答案里的一个笔误 Solutions 原文写作 "Y = 400 + 0.25Y + 500"， 这里 0.25 应为 0.75（等式左移后才出现 0.25Y = 900）。 最终答案 Y = 3600 是正确的，按上面的写法推导即可。

Section C

Section C · 讨论题（选做）

C1：45° 线模型能解释中国 90–00 年代的低通胀高增长吗？

The Keynesian 45° line assumes an abundance of underemployed economic resources (plus fixed prices/wages). China's growth in recent years has been fuelled almost entirely by growth in aggregate demand, particularly exports. China's ability to grow so rapidly without inflation reflects the almost perfectly elastic nature of SUPPLY within the economy. However, as growth continues and all resources are employed, inflation may rise and the Keynesian 45° diagram may no longer be an appropriate description.

💡 这题把 Lecture 2 与 Lecture 7 连起来了 供给近乎完全弹性 = 水平的 SAS；一旦资源用尽 ⇒ SAS 变陡直至垂直的 LAS ⇒ 需求扩张只推高价格。
C2：人口老龄化如何改变消费函数？

The longer you live, particularly beyond retirement, the more you need to save. Therefore the MPC will fall, leading to a FLATTER consumption function. However, the answer may change if people postpone retirement because of increased life expectancy. Further complications arise when consideration is given to state intervention and how pensions are financed.

C3：为什么消费函数的形状在短期与长期可能不同？

Autonomous consumption is consumption unrelated to income; households wish to consume this amount even if income is zero. In the short run this is possible by running down savings and assets. However, in the long run this level of consumption may no longer be possible.

C4：投资在多大程度上是自主支出？

According to Keynes, "animal spirits" are an important determinant of investment. But how are animal spirits affected by macroeconomic stability, consumption patterns and export growth? Investment is therefore unlikely to be autonomous. Also, even if animal spirits matter, there are surely a number of economic factors — interest rates, profits, taxation — that will have at least some influence.

💡 这正是 Lecture 5–6 要做的事 引入 I = I(r)（II 曲线）⇒ 得到向下倾斜的 IS 曲线。
C5：考虑其他漏出后乘数会怎样？

A higher rate of saving leakage leads to a lower multiplier. Therefore it is an easy step to show that a higher number of leakages (taxes and imports) will lead to a lower multiplier.

⇒ 完整乘数 k = 1/[s + ct + z]，见 Lecture 3 §10。

C6：如果家庭想减少储蓄，节俭悖论意味着什么？

An increase in autonomous consumption leads to an increase in equilibrium income. However, equilibrium saving must still equal planned investment (assumed autonomous) and is therefore unchanged.

💡 节俭悖论是对称的 想多存 ⇒ 收入下降、储蓄不变；想少存 ⇒ 收入上升、储蓄仍然不变。 变的永远是收入，不是储蓄。
