Section A · 主要练习

Q1 经典辨析

In equilibrium, desired saving equals desired investment. Is the statement true or false? Explain.

参考答案

This is TRUE, but ONLY in a closed economy with no government activity. In an open economy with government economic activity, in equilibrium desired LEAKAGES equal desired INJECTIONS.

完整层级（答题时按这个写） 一部门（仅银行）： 均衡 ⟺ planned S = planned I 两/三部门： 均衡 ⟺ S + NT + Z = I + G + X ⚠️ 另一层区分（Lecture 2 §7，很多人漏掉）： actual S ≡ actual I 是会计恒等式，永远成立 planned S = planned I 只在均衡时成立
🎯 这题的满分答案有两个层次 ① desired vs actual（Lecture 2）；② 封闭无政府 vs 开放有政府（本讲）。
只答一层最多拿一半分。

Q2 概念

Why does the government raise taxes when it could borrow to cover its spending?

参考答案

Although some government borrowing is irredeemable (i.e. perpetuities, which need never be repaid — e.g. undated Consols), most is dated and has to be repaid sometime. However interest has to be paid on ALL government borrowing. Therefore the cost of servicing the debt (repayments and interest) limits the amount the government can borrow — just as the cost of paying off the credit card limits the amount a prudent private individual can borrow.

💡 与 Lecture 3 §8 的联系 债务可持续性判据：i > π + g ⇒ 不可持续。 利息负担正是这里说的 "cost of servicing the debt"。

Q3 机制

The EU's trade partners have a recession. (a) What happens to the EU's trade balance? (b) What happens to equilibrium EU output?

参考答案

(a) If its partners experience a recession, the EU countries will find it more difficult to sell their exports. If they continue to import at the same level then the balance of external trade will be reduced and may go into deficit.

(b) Exports are an INJECTION into the circular flow, so if their value falls, other things being equal, EU output also falls.

量化ΔY = k × ΔX = ΔX / [1 − c(1−t) + z]
🎯 这题是 Lecture 11 §8 "国际商业周期传导"的微观版 本国的出口 = 他国的进口 ⇒ 衰退通过贸易渠道跨国传导。

Q4 辨析

Why are these statements wrong? (a) The Chancellor raised taxes and spending by equal amounts. It will be a neutral budget for output. (b) Government policy should balance exports and imports but ensure that the government and private sector both spend less than they earn.

参考答案

(a) The statement is wrong. Some of the tax increase may be paid by reducing savings, so consumption may be reduced by LESS than the increase in government expenditure. Hence an equal increase in both G and T can still have a positive multiplier effect on output, as given by the balanced budget multiplier.

(b) 用恒等式反驳 均衡时：S + NT + Z = I + G + X ⇒ (S − I) + (NT − G) = (X − Z) 题目要求 X = Z ⇒ 右边 = 0 ⇒ (S − I) + (NT − G) = 0 ⇒ 若私人部门有盈余（S > I），政府部门必须有等额赤字； ⇒ 反之，政府盈余必须由私人部门赤字抵消。 ⇒ 两个部门不可能同时有盈余。因此该说法是错的。

Q5 重点 选择

The trade surplus equals (a) the government surplus plus the private sector surplus (b) the government deficit plus the private sector surplus (c) the government deficit plus the private sector deficit.

参考答案

Statement (a) is correct. In equilibrium, planned leakages equal planned injections:

S + NT + Z = I + G + X (S − I) + (NT − G) = (X − Z) └私人盈余┘ └公共盈余┘ └贸易盈余┘ ⇒ trade surplus = private sector surplus + government surplus ✓ 选 (a)
🎯 这个恒等式在真题里反复出现 务必背到能随手写出并推导。三个括号从左到右依次是 private / public / foreign——顺序与 Lecture 1 Slide 19 的标注完全一致。

Q6 重点 计算

Equilibrium output in a closed economy is £1000, consumption £800, investment £80. (a) Deduce G. (b) Investment rises by £50, MPC out of national income = 0.8. New equilibrium Y, C, I, G? (c) Suppose instead G had risen by £50 — new equilibrium? (d) If potential output is £1200, to what must G rise?

参考答案
(a) Y = C + I + G ⇒ 1000 = 800 + 80 + G ⇒ G = £120 (b) 乘数 = 1/(1 − 0.8) = 5 ΔY = 5 × £50 = £250 Y = £1250；C 上升 0.8 × 250 = 200 ⇒ C = £1000 I = £130（80 + 50），G = £120（不变） 验算：1000 + 130 + 120 = 1250 ✓ (c) 效果完全相同，因为 G 与 I 都是注入， 在另一项不变时，改变哪一个的效果都一样。 Y = £1250, C = £1000, I = £80, G = £170 验算：1000 + 80 + 170 = 1250 ✓ (d) 要达到 Y = 1200：ΔY = 200 ⇒ ΔG = 200/5 = 40 Y = £1200, C = £960（0.8×1200）, I = £80, G = £160 验算：960 + 80 + 160 = 1200 ✓
💡 (c) 的结论值得记住 在简单凯恩斯模型中，ΔG 与 ΔI 的乘数完全相同（都是 1/(1−c)）。 但 ΔT 的乘数不同（绝对值 = c/(1−c)，更小）——因为多缴 1 元税只减少 c 元消费。
这个不对称正是平衡预算乘数 = 1 的来源。

Q7 重点 计算

The government spends £6 billion on rail track. The income tax rate is 0.25 and the MPC out of disposable income is 0.8. (a) Effect on equilibrium income and output? (b) Assuming the budget is in deficit, does the deficit rise or fall? Why?

参考答案
(a) 先算"对国民收入的边际消费倾向" 每 1 英镑国民收入中 0.25 被征税，可支配收入的 MPC 是 0.8： at the margin, C = 0.8 × (0.75)Y = 0.6Y 每一英镑额外国民收入的去向： £0.60 → 额外消费 £0.15 → 额外储蓄（0.2 × 0.75） £0.25 → 额外税收 合计 = 1.00 ✓ Multiplier = 1/(1 − 0.6) = 1/0.4 = 2.5 ⇒ Equilibrium income rises by 6 × 2.5 = £15bn
(b) 赤字是升是降 Taxes rise by a quarter of £15bn = £3.75bn G has risen by £6bn ⇒ 预算赤字增加 6 − 3.75 = £2.25bn
🎯 这题验证了 Lecture 3 §7 的推理链 G↑ ⇒ Y↑（乘数）⇒ 税收 tY↑，但税收的增加小于 G 的增加 ⇒ 赤字仍然扩大。
课件原文："Public sector budget deficit increases, although Y* would also raise tax revenue tY*."
💡 用 Lecture 3 的公式交叉验证 乘数 = 1/[1 − c(1−t)] = 1/[1 − 0.8(0.75)] = 1/[1 − 0.6] = 2.5 ✓
也可用 1/(s + ct) = 1/(0.2 + 0.2) = 2.5 ✓（注意本题无进口，z = 0）

Q8 结构性预算

In 2010 the new UK government wanted to reduce the enormous budget deficit, but pointed out that the structural budget deficit was significantly smaller than the actual deficit. (a) What does this mean? (b) Why does it matter? (c) Why does this make subsequent growth so important?

参考答案

(a) The structural deficit adjusts the actual deficit for deviations of actual output from its long-run equilibrium level. Like other economies, UK's output in 2010 was well below the potential output, and this had significantly reduced tax revenue at constant tax rates.

(b) If the UK's output can be restored to its long-run equilibrium level, government revenue will increase substantially EVEN WITHOUT increases in tax rates.

(c) Since debt accumulation depends on ACTUAL deficits, the faster the economy grows the more quickly the actual budget deficit will shrink and the sooner the increases in debt can be stabilised and reversed.

🎯 三问三个层次，逻辑很漂亮 (a) 是什么（定义）→ (b) 为什么重要（政策含义：不用加税）→ (c) 为什么增长关键（债务累积看的是实际赤字，不是结构性赤字）。
这种"层层递进"正是 essay 的理想结构。

Q9 进阶推导

If Y** is long-run equilibrium output and Y* is short-run equilibrium output, deduce the relationship between the marginal tax rate t, the discrepancy between actual and structural budget, and the power of the automatic stabilizers.

参考答案
乘数 = 1/[1 − (1−t)c + z] ⇒ t 越大，乘数越小。 更高的边际税率抑制乘数，起到自动（部分）稳定器的作用。 实际预算赤字 = G − T = G − tY* 结构性预算赤字 = 用 Y** 替代 Y* = G − tY** actual budget − structural budget = [G − tY*] − [G − tY**] = t(Y** − Y*) ⇒ 边际税率越大，实际预算与结构性预算之间的差距就越大， 因为收入的偏离产生了更大的影响。
💡 一句话总结这题 t 同时做两件事：① 降低乘数（稳定产出）； ② 放大实际赤字与结构性赤字的差距（使实际赤字更"周期性"）。
这两件事其实是同一枚硬币的两面——税收对收入越敏感， 它既缓冲得越多，也随周期波动得越厉害。

Q10 · Q11 评价 / 计算

Q10：债务/GDP 比率是好的负债指标吗？何时会误导？

Yes it is. Throughout most of the first decade of the 21st century, the UK's debt/GDP ratio was about 40%. Even after the Euro crisis it remained at about 60%. The debt/GDP ratio of Greece was nearly 150%.

However, debt is a STOCK and a government liability; GDP is a FLOW and a potential source of tax revenue. The economic variable that connects real stocks and real flows is the REAL INTEREST RATE. If the real interest rate is low then it is possible to service a much higher level of debt. If the real interest rate remains constant, changes in the debt/GDP ratio do tell us what is happening to government indebtedness — but if real interest rates doubled, it would take much larger GDP and tax revenue to service any given amount of debt.

🎯 "stock vs flow" 是这题的核心得分点 债务是存量，GDP 是流量，把两者相比本身就需要一个"桥梁"—— 实际利率。这与 Lecture 3 §8 的 (i − π − g)·D/(pY) 是同一件事。
Q11：什么样的 (s, t, z) 会使乘数低到 0.67？
乘数 = 1/[1 + z − (1−s)(1−t)] = 1/[z + s(1−t) + t] 要使乘数 = 0.67 = 2/3，分母必须等于 1.5。 任何使分母 = 1.5 的 (s, t, z) 组合都可以。 参考解答给的例子：z = 0.75, s = t = 0.5 验算：z + s(1−t) + t = 0.75 + 0.5(0.5) + 0.5 = 0.75 + 0.25 + 0.5 = 1.5 ✓ ⇒ 乘数 = 1/1.5 = 0.67 ✓
💡 乘数小于 1 意味着什么 意味着漏出极其严重——每 1 英镑自主支出的增加，最终只带来不到 1 英镑的产出增加。 现实中极小的开放经济（高 z）加上高税率可能接近这种情形（Lecture 3 §10 的 Belgium 例子）。

Q12 Essay

"By 2007, the UK had had over 50 consecutive quarters of steady growth… this confirms the superiority of monetary policy over fiscal policy in demand management." Is this broadly correct?

参考答案（参考解答给了七条要点，是极好的 essay 素材）

This is NOT broadly correct, but highly arguable.

论点内容
① 归因困难 Economics is a social science; it is very difficult to isolate all the variables and say with certainty that an outcome results from one particular variable. This is why economists use "other things being equal" (ceteris paribus).
② 其他可能的原因 支持 1997 年下台的保守党政府的人认为经济是被他们的政策留在良好状态的； 可以指出 Gordon Brown 头几年沿用了保守党的支出计划（财政政策）。 其他观察者把持续增长归因于良性的世界环境——除日本外多数经济体都在稳步增长， 对英国这样的贸易国家来说条件理想。
③ 央行独立的效果也难以归因 把货币政策决策权从财政部转给英格兰银行的原因之一是效仿德国。 但德国的增长同样不能确定地归因于央行独立性。
不过可以肯定的是：在英国，货币与财政政策曾被用来赢得选举而非维持经济稳定， 这可能产生了不稳定效果（呼应 Lecture 11 的政治性商业周期）。
④ 灵活性的确是货币政策的优势 货币政策确实比财政政策灵活得多（英国财政政策通常一年只变一次， 除非有 2010 年那样的紧急预算）。
但由于各种时滞（recognition, implementation, effects）， 货币与财政政策都需要 18 个月到两年才能影响实体经济。
⑤ 货币政策的一个劣势 政府无法控制央行释放的额外货币流向何处。 早期扩张性货币政策推高了房产价格而非增加产出；
2007 年后，商业银行似乎把额外的钱用于修补自己的资本状况而非贷给企业， 大量资金流向了股票与债券市场而非实体经济。
⑥ 凯恩斯的经典论点 "Monetary policy can be used to make money available, but the public cannot be FORCED to borrow it." 除非家庭与企业对经济前景有信心，否则不会借贷。 在这种情况下财政政策就有作用可发挥。
⑦ 财政政策的独特优势 减税同样存在"政府无法控制额外购买力流向"的问题——可能被用于额外进口或出国度假， 两者都无助于国内经济。
但政府对自己的支出有控制权，可以主动启动项目： 在萧条地区创造就业、改善基础设施、发展环境可持续能源等。
🎯 结论怎么写 不是"货币政策优于财政政策"，而是"两者角色不同"： 常态下货币政策更灵活、更适合微调；但在流动性陷阱、信心崩塌、零利率下限的情形下 （2007 年后正是如此），货币政策失灵，财政政策不可或缺。
参考解答最后一句："the events of 2007, along with the current concern over environmental sustainability, affect the answer."

B3 重点 平衡预算乘数

封闭经济：I = 450（固定），消费 = 可支配收入的 80%，初始 G = 250， 直接税 = 收入的 10%。然后 G 增加 500，税率由 10% 升到 25%。

参考答案（七小问全解）
(a) 初始均衡收入 假设自主消费 A = 0： Y = (I + G) / [1 − c(1 − t)] = (450 + 250) / [1 − 0.8(1 − 0.1)] = 700 / [1 − 0.72] = 700 / 0.28 = 2,500
(b) 消费支出 C = 0.8 × 2,500 × 0.9 = 1,800 税收收入 = 0.1 × 2,500 = 250 政府预算 = tY − G = 250 − 250 = 0（平衡）
(c) 产出尚未调整时，可支配收入减少多少 原可支配收入 = 2,500 × 0.9 = 2,250 现可支配收入 = 2,500 × 0.75 = 1,875 ⇒ 减少 375
(d) 消费与总需求的变化 消费下降 = 0.80 × 375 = 300 总需求增加 = 500（G 的增加）− 300（消费的下降）= 200
(e) 新的均衡收入 新分母 = 1 − 0.8(1 − 0.25) = 1 − 0.6 = 0.4 新的 I + G = 450 + 750 = 1,200 Y = 1,200 / 0.4 = 3,000
(f) 新的政府预算 tY − G = 0.25 × 3,000 − 750 = 750 − 750 = 0（仍然平衡）
(g) 平衡预算乘数 Multiplier = ΔY / ΔG = 500 / 500 = 1 In this case, the multiplier is UNITY.
🎯 这就是"平衡预算乘数 = 1"的完整数值证明 预算前后都是平衡的（0 → 0），但产出从 2,500 上升到 3,000， 增加量恰好等于 ΔG = 500。
直觉：ΔG 全额进入 AD；增税只减少 c 倍的消费。 净扩张 = (1 − c)ΔG，乘以乘数 1/(1−c) ⇒ ΔY = ΔG。
政策含义（C4 参考答案）：这给了政府一个"不增加赤字或债务就能提振 AD"的财政工具。

B7 重点 图

图中 AB 与 CD 分别是有/无外贸的 AD 曲线（进口与收入成比例，出口外生）。 (a) 哪条是有外贸的？(b) 无外贸时的均衡收入？(c) 有外贸时的均衡收入？(d) 贸易余额为零的收入？ (e) 外贸使乘数变大还是变小？

参考答案

(a) AB is the aggregate demand schedule WITHOUT foreign trade. Adding autonomous exports together with imports proportional to income moves the schedule to CD。

(b) OG（无外贸时的均衡收入）

(c) OF（有外贸时的均衡收入）

(d) OE — OE corresponds to the point at which exports = imports. At this point, aggregate demand is the SAME on both AB and CD, as net exports are equal to zero.

(e) The multiplier is REDUCED by foreign trade through the effect of the marginal propensity to import.

💡 (d) 的判别技巧值得记住 两条 AD 曲线相交的那一点，就是净出口 = 0 的收入水平。
因为 CD = AB + (X − zY)，两线相交 ⟺ X − zY = 0 ⟺ X = Z。
在这一点左边 X > Z（贸易盈余，CD 在 AB 上方）；右边 X < Z（贸易赤字，CD 在 AB 下方）。
⇒ CD 比 AB 更平坦（斜率小了 z），这正是乘数变小的图形表现。

B9B (False) 开放经济处于均衡并不意味着贸易赤字为零。
均衡条件是 S + NT + Z = I + G + X，贸易余额可以是任何值， 只要被其他部门的余额抵消。（呼应 Lecture 3 §9：贸易平衡的收入水平 "not necessarily at full employment"）
B10C 更高的出口需求 increases 产出（X 是注入）；更高的 MPZ reduces 产出 （z 增大 ⇒ 乘数变小）。
Section C

Section C · 讨论题（选做）

C1：如果美国家庭储蓄率为零，政府支出增加会有什么影响？

If the marginal propensity to save is zero (at all levels of income) then, in the absence of any other leakages (taxation and imports), the multiplier is INFINITE! An increase in government spending would have an enormous (infinite) impact on aggregate demand. But the MPS is unlikely to be zero for all levels of income. Note also that the question refers to the household saving RATE — this might well be the AVERAGE saving rate rather than the MARGINAL rate, and a zero average rate does not necessarily imply a zero marginal rate.

🎯 "平均 vs 边际"这一区分是本题的关键得分点 乘数取决于 MPS（边际），不是 APS（平均）。
由 S = −A + (1−c)Y，APS = S/Y = −A/Y + (1−c)， 在 Y = A/(1−c) 处 APS = 0，但此时 MPS 仍是 (1−c) > 0。
C2：如果美国有巨大的贸易失衡，答案会怎样改变？

If the US has a significant marginal propensity to import (not quite the same as a large trade imbalance with the rest of the world), then this reduces the value of the government expenditure multiplier.

💡 注意参考解答的括号提醒 "贸易失衡的规模"与"边际进口倾向"是两回事。 乘数取决于 z（边际），不取决于贸易余额的水平。
C3：国债可以增长多快？

As for individuals who take on loans and mortgages, the government's ability to borrow is based on their ability to pay. The national debt can therefore grow at, or around, a similar rate to GDP. Additional consideration can be given to taxation rates, printing money and worries regarding inflation. There may be a maximum feasible value of the GDP-debt ratio; if so, the government can allow the national debt to grow faster than GDP until this ceiling is reached, but then it is constrained to grow no faster than GDP.

C4：平衡预算乘数的含义是什么？

The balanced budget multiplier says that a rise in government spending accompanied by an equal rise in taxes leads to an increase in output. This is because the tax rise reduces consumption by LESS than the increase in government spending, because the MPC is less than one. Aggregate demand then rises and increases output, resulting in increased consumption demand.

This gives the government a fiscal tool to boost aggregate demand WITHOUT adding to the deficit or debt. However, to use this tool, the government does have to have the political courage to raise tax revenue in line with higher expenditure. Sometimes governments are unable or unwilling to do this.

C5：为什么政府难以用财政政策完全消除繁荣与萧条？

In theory the government can stabilise AD by cutting taxes, raising spending, or both. However:

  • 时滞：it takes time for the government to SPOT that aggregate demand has changed; then it also takes time for fiscal policy to have an EFFECT.
  • 不确定性：the government faces uncertainty over the size of the multiplier and the subsequent changes in the other components of AD.
  • 抵消性效应：the increase in G may be offset by changes in other components of AD — for example, in Japan in the 1990s expansionary fiscal policy fuelled private sector pessimism and reductions in consumption and investment.
🎯 这三条正是 Lecture 3 §6 的三个局限 Time lags · Uncertainty · Induced effects on autonomous demand。 日本 1990 年代的例子是第三条的绝佳实证素材。
C6：英国实行的是中性的结构性预算政策吗？

The government currently aims to balance its budget OVER THE ECONOMIC CYCLE. Therefore the answer is yes: the government has a NEUTRAL stance, at least over the economic cycle.

Exam Technique

✍️ 本次 Tutorial 的答题结构点评

题型 1：恒等式推理题（A4, A5, B1, B9）

固定三步

① 先写出均衡条件：S + NT + Z = I + G + X；
② 重排成三部门余额形式：(S − I) + (NT − G) = (X − Z)；
③ 代入题目的约束条件（如 X = Z ⇒ 右边 = 0），推出结论。

这三步几乎可以解掉所有这类题。不要凭直觉答，一定要把恒等式写出来。

题型 3：赤字与财政立场（A7(b), A8, A9, A10）

这类题的核心永远是区分"内生的周期性成分"与"外生的政策成分"：

实际赤字 = G − tY*（含周期成分） 结构性赤字 = G − tY**（用潜在产出，剔除周期） 两者之差 = t(Y** − Y*) ⇒ 答题模板："The actual deficit is a poor indicator of the fiscal stance because it is endogenous to the cycle. The structural (cyclically adjusted) deficit isolates the discretionary component."

题型 4：Essay（A12）

A12 的参考答案是七条并列论点，注意它没有简单站队，而是： ①质疑归因（社会科学难以隔离变量）→ ②给出替代解释 → ③承认对方论点的合理部分（灵活性）→ ④指出对方论点的漏洞（时滞同样存在、货币政策无法控制资金流向、无法强迫借贷）→ ⑤给出财政政策不可替代的场景。

💡 这个结构可以套用到任何 "Discuss / Is this correct?" 题 不要一上来就说"对"或"错"。先说 "This is not broadly correct, but highly arguable" 这类有保留的判断，再逐条展开，最后给出有条件的结论（"in normal times… but in a liquidity trap…"）。
